SUMMARY

Designed for real workflows, not edge cases.

Humanly is a centralised HR platform designed to simplify everyday workflows for employees and HR teams.

It brings payroll, time-off, incidents and internal resources into one calm, reliable experience.

02 RESEARCH & INSIGHTS

Research revealed high context-switching, low task visibility and frustration around simple but frequent actions.

56% Employees struggled to find the correct information for basic HR tasks.
47% Requests required manual HR intervention due to unclear flows or missing data.
31% Payroll-related issues were caused by incorrect or incomplete employee input.
03 PERSONAS & USER NEEDS

Two primary user groups were identified, each with very different expectations.

EMPLOYEE

Age: 25-45 Role: Full-time employee across different departments.

Frustrations

  • Not knowing where to find specific information.
  • Fear of making mistakes that affect payroll or time-off.

Needs

  • Clear navigation and plain language.
  • Visibility over personal data and requests.

Goals

Complete everyday HR tasks quickly and independently, with clear feedback and full confidence in the outcome.

HR ADMIN

Age: 30-55 Role: HR manager or People Operations specialist.

Frustrations

  • Repetitive manual tasks.
  • Managing approvals across disconnected tools.

Needs

  • Clear overview and prioritization.
  • Control without micromanaging.

Goals

Maintain accurate data and efficient workflows while reducing manual work and preventable errors.

STRUCTURAL TENSION

The core tension wasn’t usability. It was control vs autonomy.

HR needed:

  • Structured data
  • Fewer errors
  • Approval oversight
  • Compliance safety

Employees wanted:

  • Speed
  • Clarity
  • Fewer steps
  • Independence

This created friction:

The more control HR added, the more complex the flows became. The simpler the experience for employees, the less visibility HR initially felt they had.

The challenge wasn’t just simplifying screens. It was designing a system where autonomy didn’t compromise accuracy.

04 USER FLOWS & IA

Key flows were designed to reduce steps, prevent errors and remove unnecessary decisions.

If something can be misunderstood, it eventually will.

01 Request time-off

User Employee
Problem Employees lacked clarity on available days, policies and approval status.
Design approach Surface remaining balance upfront and clarify the approval flow before submission.
Outcome
↓ Clarification requests
↑ Approval speed

02 View payslip

User Employee
Problem Payslips were hard to find and difficult to understand.
Design approach Centralize access and present a clear breakdown of earnings and deductions.
Outcome
↑ User confidence
↓ Payroll-related questions

03 Payroll approval

User HR Admin
Problem Approvals were delayed due to missing or incorrect employee data.
Design approach Flag incomplete entries and support faster reviews with structured checklists and batch actions.
Outcome
↓ Processing time
↓ Approval errors

04 Clock in / out

User Employee
Problem Inconsistent tracking led to errors and manual payroll corrections.
Design approach Enable one-tap actions with clear feedback, visibility and gentle reminders.
Outcome
↑ Tracking accuracy
↓ Admin corrections

Flows were simplified to reduce steps, ambiguity and approval bottlenecks.

These flows were validated against the full employee journey to ensure consistency across touchpoints.

WHAT WAS DIFFICULT?
01

On how much visibility employees should have into payroll breakdowns. HR initially preferred limited visibility to reduce “questions”. We pushed for transparency to increase trust and reduce repetitive inquiries.

02

On approval flows. Some stakeholders wanted more validation layers “just in case”. We argued that excessive safeguards were causing more delays than protection.

A CONTROVERSIAL DECISION

We intentionally removed certain intermediate confirmation steps in time-off and payroll flows. This reduced friction and completion time, but initially raised concerns about error risk. Instead of adding more steps, we introduced:

– Inline validation – Contextual warnings – Structured input fields
THE RESULT

Fewer steps, fewer mistakes, faster approvals.
