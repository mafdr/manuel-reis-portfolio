ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm a UX/UI Designer based in Lisbon with 9 years of experience designing digital products.

I enjoy working across the whole design process, from understanding the problem and mapping flows, to designing interfaces and prototyping ideas. Being involved end-to-end helps me keep things simple and make sure the final product actually makes sense for the people using it.

Outside of design, I spend my time reading, cycling around the city, experimenting with clay, or playing video games. I grew up with games like The Legend of Zelda, which probably explains why I still love well-crafted worlds, thoughtful systems, and good interaction design.
